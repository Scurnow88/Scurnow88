## X-Clash Hero Reference

See [`x-clash/X-CLASH-HERO-GUIDE.md`](x-clash/X-CLASH-HERO-GUIDE.md) for the full tier list, upgrade guide, and team comps.

**CSV / Excel files** (import to Google Sheets or open in Excel):
- `x-clash/X-CLASH-MASTER-RAW-DATA.xlsx` — **master workbook** (My Teams, My Roster, heroes, skills)
- `x-clash/x-clash-my-teams.csv` — your locked Union Boss / World Boss / PvP lineups
- `x-clash/x-clash-my-roster.csv` — your owned heroes with CP, skills, team assignments
- `x-clash/x-clash-heroes-master.csv` — all 31 heroes with roles & tiers
- `x-clash/x-clash-heroes-skills.csv` — skills (update from in-game screenshots)
- `x-clash/x-clash-heroes-tier-by-mode.csv` — PvP / World Boss / Campaign tiers
- `x-clash/x-clash-my-roster-template.csv` — blank template for new heroes

Regenerate the workbook after CSV edits: `python3 x-clash/scripts/generate-master-workbook.py`
