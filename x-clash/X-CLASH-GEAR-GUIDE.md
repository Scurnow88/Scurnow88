# X-Clash Gear Guide

## Two Different Gear Systems in Our Sheets

| System | Names in old sheet | What it actually is |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Hero Clash Artifacts** | Cursed Book, Devil's Heart, Angelic Headband… | Special **artifact slots** from the parent game (Hero Clash). X-Clash may use different names — check your hero's **Artifact** tab if it exists. |
| **X-Clash Craft Gear** | **Destiny Sword / Armour / Helmet / Greaves** | **Craftable legendary gear** from the in-game **Craft Gear** menu (your screenshots). Upgrade each piece to **Lv40**. |

> **Use Destiny gear** for concrete build targets. Treat artifact names as *role guides* (DPS vs tank vs support) until we map exact X-Clash artifact names from your screenshots.

## Destiny Set (Legendary) — Verified Jul 20 2026

Full stats: [`x-clash-gear-destiny-set.csv`](x-clash-gear-destiny-set.csv)

### Recommended by role

| Role | Weapon | Chest | Head | Boots |
|------|--------|-------|------|-------|
| **Magic DPS** (Sera, Monica, Fenixia) | Destiny Sword | Destiny Armour *or* highest HP chest | Destiny Helmet | Destiny Greaves |
| **Physical DPS** (Verna, Marissa, Alvarez) | Destiny Sword | Destiny Armour | Destiny Helmet | Destiny Greaves |
| **Tank** (Andrew, Dragonic) | Destiny Sword | Destiny Armour | Destiny Helmet | Destiny Greaves |
| **Support** (Yord, Denise) | Destiny Helmet or Sword | Destiny Armour | Destiny Helmet | Destiny Greaves |

### Why this set works

- **Destiny Sword** — highest ATK + Crit Rate scaling (best DPS weapon craft line)
- **Destiny Armour** — massive HP + **Physical DMG reduction** at Lv20/Lv40
- **Destiny Helmet** — ATK + **General DMG reduction** (good for everyone)
- **Destiny Greaves** — HP/DEF + **Magic DMG reduction** (critical for Andrew)

### Craft cost (per piece)

- **750** orange crystals
- **495K** gold
- Upgrade milestones unlock at **Lv10 / Lv20 / Lv30 / Lv40**

## Substats to chase (when reforging/random stats appear)

| Role | Priority substats |
|------|-------------------|
| **DPS** | ATK → Crit Rate → Skill DMG → CRIT DMG |
| **Tank** | HP → DMG Reduction → DEF |
| **Support** | HP → Speed → Buff effect / DMG Reduction |
| **World Boss** | ATK → Monster DMG → Crit Rate |

## Your investment order (with Destiny gear)

1. **Craft Destiny Sword** for main DPS (**Sera** first)
2. **Craft Destiny Helmet** for **Yord** (survivability + ATK)
3. **Craft Destiny Greaves + Armour** for **Andrew** when he hits 5★
4. Fill remaining slots on **Dragonic** / **Verna** as you farm crystals
