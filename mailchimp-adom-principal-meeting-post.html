Diocese of Arlington Principals’ Meeting

Thank you for
a meaningful morning together.

It was a pleasure to join school leaders at St. Andrew Catholic School on Tuesday, August 4. Archangel Education + Technology is grateful for the opportunity to be part of the Diocese of Arlington Principals’ Meeting and to hear what matters most to your school communities as the new year begins.

If we had the chance to connect at our table, thank you for sharing what is on your mind for the coming school year—whether that was classroom technology, instructional planning, device refresh, or simply thinking ahead about what your teachers and students may need. We would be glad to continue the conversation and offer our support as you plan ahead.

If we did not get to connect in person, we would still welcome the chance to learn more about your school and how Archangel can support you. Desire Vanterpool, our Technology Advisor, is happy to speak by phone or email whenever it is convenient for you.

As we shared at the meeting, our approach begins with listening. Every school community is different, and our goal is to understand your mission, instructional priorities, and unique needs before recommending any technology solution.
